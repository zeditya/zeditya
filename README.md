## How I work

<img align="right" width="270" src="assets/lock-in.webp" alt="Leaning forward, locked in" />

- **Consistency over intensity.** Ninety focused minutes beats an all-nighter I need a weekend to recover from.
- **Read the whole error.** The answer is usually in the part I skipped.
- **Finish things.** Deployed and slightly ugly beats perfect and private.
